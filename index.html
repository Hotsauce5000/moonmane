Moonmane

A pocket horse on a 32×20 dot screen. Hatch the egg, then raise your foal: feed it, muck out the stall, call the vet, and turn off the barn lights when it naps.

Buttons

  • A
  • Move to the next icon. In menus, move the arrow. In Gallop, guess left. A or 1
  • B
  • Use the lit icon or pick the menu item. In Gallop, guess right. B or 2
  • C
  • Back out. With no icon lit, show the clock. C or 3

The eight icons

    Egg → Foal → Yearling → Arabian, Pony or Mule. Fewer care mistakes as a yearling make an Arabian. A mistake is leaving a call unanswered for 45 seconds or letting sickness drag on. Once grown, a horse year passes every 15 minutes. Close the page and time keeps going for up to 20 minutes, but your horse comes to no harm while you're away.
